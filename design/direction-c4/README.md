# C4 Lantern Room

Silk Thread layout, kid-forward for ages 3–4, no comic styling. Distinct from C and from the puddle: Comfortaa, honey mesh, paper lanterns, lamp-shaped beads, a sheen on the headline, firefly dots. No halftone, no ink borders, no speech balloons.

## Motion

- MeshGradient speed 0.9, 1.6 on celebration.
- Lucy travels a small side-to-side loop over 3.4s. Trail pearls bobble, staggered.
- Letters trickle in, 55ms apart. Five lanterns climb and sway over 7s. A soft sheen crosses the headline every 2.6s.
- Firefly dots rise on a canvas. Iris wipe. Confetti on the bow.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is an opt-out only.

## References

Used as rulebooks or redrawn in vanilla: superdesign-skill, superdesign, better-design, design-taste playful color (retro-print in that repo was not applied; this plate has no comic ink), canvas-ui firefly field, paper-design/shaders, uilayouts soft cards, hyperiux milky rising specks, motion-primitives text shimmer and float, trickle-UI-kit letter stagger (slower and with a small tilt, so it is not the puddle hop).

**Skipped:** [cursify](https://github.com/ui-layouts/cursify). Cursor-follow effects need a pointer. This product is touched with a finger.
