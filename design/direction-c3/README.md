# C3 Puddle Room

Silk Thread layout, kid-forward for ages 3–4, no comic styling. No ink outlines, halftone, speech balloons, or bursts. Fredoka, sky-and-sand mesh, round beads, ripple rings, a drifting spotlight, rising bubbles.

## Motion

- MeshGradient speed 1.05, 1.8 on celebration.
- Letters trickle with a small hop. SATPIN beads hop in a wave, 120ms apart.
- Three ripple rings expand every 3.2s. A warm spotlight wanders over 8s.
- Bubbles rise on a canvas. The trail stroke marches. Lucy’s oval squashes at the top of each float.
- Iris wipe. Confetti on the bow.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is an opt-out only.

## References

Used as rulebooks or redrawn in vanilla: superdesign-skill, superdesign, better-design, design-taste playful color (the retro-print notes in that repo were set aside so this plate would stay free of comic ink), canvas-ui bubble field, paper-design/shaders, uilayouts round plates, hyperiux rising particles, motion-primitives spotlight and text rise, trickle-UI-kit letter stagger.

**Skipped:** [cursify](https://github.com/ui-layouts/cursify). A cursor trail is the wrong accent for a touch tablet used by 3- and 4-year-olds.
