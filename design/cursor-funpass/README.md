# Cursor fun pass

Two Little Woof worlds, built on the Letter Post and Moonboat play loop. Open `index.html` from a static server. Nothing here touches `app/`.

- [Lucy’s Letter Post](v1-letter-post/index.html) — Air mail, Candy post, Dark
- [Lucy’s Moonboat](v3-moonboat/index.html) — Dark, Cloud day, Plum dusk

`?shot=1` settles a screen for a still. `?demo=1` plays the short motion path. `?theme=0|1|2` picks a theme. `?screen=home|listen|match|celebrate` opens one screen. `?hint=1` shows the two-miss help. `?visited=S,A,T` fills today’s collection for that visit only.

Sounds other than Lucy’s recordings are short tones made in the browser. They follow mute and stop when her voice starts. Reduced motion shows the finished poses and skips particles.
