# Audio licences

`music-loop.wav` and the UI hits `sfx-tap.wav`, `sfx-select.wav`,
`sfx-right.wav`, `sfx-wrong.wav`, `sfx-star.wav`, `sfx-pop.wav`, and
`sfx-woof.wav` are original beds written by `make-ui-bed.py` and released
**CC0 1.0 Universal**. 22.05 kHz, mono, 16-bit.

`sfx-cheer.wav` is ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2 (`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`),
converted to 22.05 kHz mono WAV and peak-normalized to match the other
hits. Instrumental xylophone chime. No speech. Running `make-ui-bed.py`
does not replace this file.

Phoneme / name / word recordings, when they land, must be files Bradley
drops in — never generated here as English speech.

Never scrape Logic of English, Starfall, or other commercial phonics sites.

Until a `phoneme-*` clip is mapped in `data/audio.json`, listen-round is
**silence plus Lucy’s on-screen line**. Isolated sounds are never TTS.
