## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an enchanting, confidence-building digital universe tailored for early childhood education (ages 3–7) and their co-viewing guardians. The visual language balances arcade-grade kinetic delight with calm, focused legibility. Children should feel a sense of boundless wonder, agency, and celebrated progression, while parents and educators experience reassuring stability and intuitive structural safety.

The design movement is **Tactile Toy-Arcade Skeuomorphism** blended with **Cosmic Fantasy Minimalism**:
- **Chunky 3D Tactility:** Interactive targets emulate real-world tactile arcade buttons—extruded shapes with physical push-states, light-reflecting pill bevels, thick borders, and heavy drop shadows that invite direct physical interaction.
- **Cosmic Depth:** Floating, cloud-like floating islands, glowing dust paths, and deep oceanic-night atmospheres prevent sensory overwhelm by offering deep contrast behind hyper-saturated interactive game pieces.
- **Friendly Companionship:** Dedicated mascot framing anchors interactions, ensuring Lucy the Golden Retriever can guide, react, and emote without obscuring primary learning pathways.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast jewel tones situated over vast midnight cosmic depths. Rather than a flat black, the background utilizes rich indigo-to-midnight blue gradients, lending warmth and safety to the dark mode canvas.

### Palette Roles
- **Atmospheric Neutrals:**
  - Canvas Deep: `#0f1738` (Deep Royal Navy)
  - Canvas Elevated: `#182258` (Cosmic Midnight Blue)
  - Surface Glass/Card: `#1f2d70` with soft alpha overlays for frosted containers.
  - Text White: `#ffffff` for ultra-crisp contrast on dark backgrounds.
  - Text Soft Milk: `#cbd5e1` for secondary guidance and parental controls.
- **Electric Cyan (Primary):** `#0ea5e9` with highlight `#38bdf8` and tactile base shadow `#0284c7`. Used for primary progression tracks, Math paths, and major active states.
- **Sunbeam Gold (Secondary / Rewards):** `#f59e0b` with bright glow core `#fbbf24` and base shadow `#d97706`. Reserved for stars, treasure chests, coin balances, and celebrated streaks.
- **Energetic Coral (Tertiary / Energy):** `#f43f5e` with highlight `#fb7185` and base shadow `#e11d48`. Applied to urgency prompts, game arenas, favorite milestones, and celebratory bursts.
- **Emerald Meadow (Accent / Correct Answers):** `#10b981` with base shadow `#059669`. Denotes successful lesson completions and correct selections.

Colors must consistently employ a dual-tone "body + shadow base" construction to reinforce the toy-like bevels and physical depth.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered specifically for early readers, emerging decoders, and toddlers. All letterforms prioritize distinct character anatomy (such as single-story 'a' and rounded, open terminals) to avoid confusion.

- **Headline Font (`Comfortaa`):** Geometric, organic, and ultra-rounded. Used across level titles, major milestone announcements, and arcade zone banners. Its soft loops and circular counters keep the interface friendly, approachable, and devoid of sharp, intimidating corners.
- **Body Font (`Quicksand`):** Highly legible, friendly sans-serif offering proportional spacing and clear baseline rhythms. Used for instructions, spoken-word captions, and bite-sized stories.
- **Label Font (`Comfortaa`):** Maintains the rounded, tactile identity inside big, chunky interactive push-buttons, navigation pods, and floating reward badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around the **Chunky Spatial Sandbox** model. Rather than tight grids filled with text-dense widgets, the viewport presents interactive stages with generous thumb and finger clearance.

### Spatial Principles
- **Minimum Tap Target of 88px (`target-min` / `5.5rem`):** All primary interactive arcade controls (answering choices, navigation stations, modal confirmations) must span at least 88×88px to accommodate developing gross and fine motor skills.
- **Floating Island Progression Paths:** Content flows horizontally or in an organic winding S-curve across puffy floating cloud islands. Islands are padded generously (`padding-island`) to prevent accidental mis-taps.
- **Mascot Anchor Zone:** A reserved responsive viewport region (bottom-left or bottom-right of viewport) sized at 140px on mobile and 220px on desktop/tablet, providing Lucy the Golden Retriever with a stable staging pedestal that stays clear of mission tiles.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Mobile Handheld (`< 768px`):** Single-column vertical path winding through cosmic clouds. Global navigation docks to a sticky, chunky bottom dock with oversized, stacked icons and labels.
- **Tablet / Touch Landscape (`768px – 1024px`):** Ideal play experience. Navigation shifts to a left-anchored vertical rail or persistent bottom arc. Map paths split across organic floating clusters with Lucy pinned to the dynamic foreground corner.
- **Desktop / Smart TV Display (`> 1024px`):** Content centers inside a 1280px max-width boundary floating amidst ambient cosmic particles, stars, and nebula gradients.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth does not rely on realistic real-world shadows, but rather on **Chunky 3D Toy Extrusions** and **Bioluminescent Cosmic Glows**.

### The 3D Extrusion Engine
Every interactive component possesses physical thickness created through multi-stop solid drop-borders:
- **Resting Elevation:** Elements feature a solid lower border or non-blurred offset shadow (4px to 8px) rendered in a darker shade of the element's primary surface (e.g., `#0ea5e9` surface with `#0284c7` bottom bevel).
- **Active / Pressed Elevation:** When tapped, the element translates downward along the Y-axis by `4px` or `6px`, and its bottom shadow compresses to `0px` or `2px`, delivering immediate tactile satisfaction akin to physical toy buttons.

### Cosmic Glows & Atmospheric Layers
- **Tier 1 (Atmosphere):** Deep linear/radial gradient from `#0f1738` to `#182258` populated with twinkling ambient stars.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Islands & Cloud Paths):** Puffy cloud platforms using translucent glass layers with bright white/ice borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Tier 3 (Toy Elements & Badges):** High-opacity vibrant solids with hard-edge extrusion rims.
- **Tier 4 (Glows & Celebrations):** Multi-layered diffused luminescence (`0 0 24px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.55)`) applied exclusively to stars, streaks, unlocked portals, and reward chests.

## Shapes

The design system exclusively uses organic, ultra-soft geometries (`roundedness: 3`) to evoke inflatable toys, puffy cloud formations, and friendly arcade consoles.

- **Pill & Capsule Shells:** All action triggers, chips, and progress meters feature fully rounded terminal pill caps (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Cloud Containers:** Surface cards and dialogue speech bubbles leverage asymmetric soft curves (`32px` to `48px` border radius) combined with circular pseudo-elements to produce genuine cloud billows.
- **Inset Highlights:** Prominent buttons and badges feature an internal top-inset highlight arc (a white translucent sliver at `opacity: 0.35`) simulating the shiny gleam of plastic or polished rubber.

## Components

### 1. Chunky 3D Arcade Buttons
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped, minimum height 88px for standard triggers; 64px for secondary parent actions. Top glossy sheen + thick 6px bottom bevel border in saturated contrasting shade.
- **Variations:**
  - *Electric Cyan (Primary Action):* Surface `#0ea5e9`, bevel edge `#0284c7`, text `#ffffff`.
  - *Sunbeam Gold (Rewards/Play):* Surface `#fbbf24`, bevel edge `#d97706`, text `#78350f`.
  - *Energetic Coral (Games/Hot):* Surface `#f43f5e`, bevel edge `#be123c`, text `#ffffff`.
- **States:** Hover lifts +2px; active press translates translateY(4px) and collapses bottom bevel.

### 2. Main Navigation Pods (Bottom Dock / Side Rail)
- **Structure:** Chunky floating bar holding large rounded icon pods: *Home*, *Math 123*, *ABC English*, *Library*, *Games*.
- **Visuals:** Each pod contains an expressive 3D-styled vector icon resting in an illuminated circular cavity. Active tab pops upward with a glowing under-pill highlight and bouncing star particles.

### 3. Puffy Cloud Progression Map
- **Structure:** Interconnected cloud platforms layered over the cosmic midnight backdrop.
- **Nodes:** Large circular stepping stones (minimum 88px diameter) with numbered steps or letter goals. 
  - *Completed:* Golden yellow with glowing halo and animated floating stars.
  - *Current Active:* Pulsing electric cyan with bouncing mascot indicator.
  - *Locked:* Semi-transparent cosmic navy with a friendly soft padlock.

### 4. Reward & Streak Counters (Star Pods)
- **Structure:** Pill container with frosted dark-navy glass backing, framed by a thick 3px border.
- **Visuals:** Features an oversized 3D spinning gold star overflowing the left edge of the pill, followed by bold numbers in `Comfortaa 700` (`#fbbf24`).

### 5. Mascot Anchor Space (Lucy the Golden Retriever)
- **Structure:** Dedicated circular or cloud-trimmed pedestal frame anchored dynamically in the viewport.
- **Behaviors:** Houses animated vector Lucy. Supports reactive state attachments:
  - *Encouragement bubble:* Puffy speech cloud pointing directly to Lucy's snout.
  - *Celebration:* Confetti bursts, tail wags, and star tosses on correct quiz answers.

### 6. Interactive Answer Cards
- **Structure:** Squircle or cloud-card formats (min height 100px) with 4px borders.
- **States:** Default dark-blue surface; on select transforms into electric cyan with soft interior pulse; on correct verification explodes into emerald green with high-contrast checkmarks and audio chime triggers.
